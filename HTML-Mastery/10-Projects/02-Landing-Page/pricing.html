Pricing FAQ

Questions about plans & billing

Is there really a free plan?

Yes. Starter is free forever for teams of up to 10 members, with 3 active projects and community support. We make money on Pro and Enterprise — not by bait-and-switching small teams.

How does per-user pricing work?

Pro is billed per active member per month (or per year on annual billing). You only pay for humans with edit access — read-only viewers, guests, and automation bots are always free.

What counts as an "active member"?

Anyone who has logged in and edited at least one task in the last 30 days. Inactive members are automatically paused from billing — no manual cleanup needed.

Can I switch between monthly and annual?

Yes, anytime. Upgrading to annual applies a prorated credit for the remaining monthly period. Downgrading back to monthly takes effect at the end of your current annual cycle.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

We offer 50% off Pro for the first year to eligible early-stage startups (under $2M raised) and 30% off for registered nonprofits. Reach out via our contact page and we'll get you set up.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit cards via Stripe, ACH transfer for annual plans, and wire/invoice for Enterprise contracts above $25k/year. We can also accept purchase orders from qualified organizations.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You keep read-only access for 90 days after cancellation, with full export to CSV, JSON, and Markdown. After 90 days we permanently delete your workspace — no dark patterns, no retention lock-in.
