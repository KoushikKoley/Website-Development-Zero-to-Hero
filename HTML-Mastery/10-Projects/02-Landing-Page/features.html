Boards & views

See your work however your brain works.

Switch between Kanban, list, timeline, calendar, and workload views without ever leaving the page. Same tasks, different lens — pick the one that helps you decide what to do next.

  • Drag-and-drop with 60fps animations on every device.
  • Save any view as a team template in two clicks.
  • Group, filter, and sort across more than 40 fields.
  • Public read-only share links for stakeholders.

Automation

Let the busywork run itself.

Build no-code rules with triggers, conditions, and chained actions. Auto-assign work, post updates, set due dates, and move cards — without writing a single line of glue code.

  • 40+ triggers and 60+ actions out of the box.
  • Branch with conditions and pause for human approval.
  • Run history with full audit trail of every action taken.
  • Reusable rule templates your whole team can clone.

AI sprint planner

Plans that respect your team's actual capacity.

TaskFlow reads your backlog, your team's velocity, and PTO calendar, then drafts a realistic sprint plan in seconds. Tune it by dragging tasks; the model learns what your team tends to bump.

  • Draft, edit, and finalize a sprint in under 5 minutes.
  • Considers capacity, holidays, and on-call rotations.
  • Flags risky scope before the sprint starts.
  • Your data never trains a shared model. Ever.
