About

I turn fuzzy product ideas into accessible interfaces people love.

For nearly a decade I've worked at the seam between design and engineering — translating Figma frames into production-grade React, polishing motion to 60fps, and writing the design tokens that keep whole teams consistent.

I care about the unglamorous parts: semantics, performance budgets, keyboard navigation, and color contrast. The result is software that loads fast, works for everyone, and feels calm to use.
