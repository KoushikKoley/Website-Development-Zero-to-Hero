FAQ

Questions I get a lot

What does a typical freelance engagement look like?

Most engagements start with a paid discovery sprint (1–2 weeks) where I audit what you have, talk to your team, and produce a written recommendation. If we both feel good about it, we move into a 6–12 week build with weekly check-ins and a shared Linear board. I work in fixed-scope sprints so you always know what you're getting and when.

Do you offer accessibility audits?

Yes. I run a two-pass audit — automated (axe-core, Lighthouse) plus manual keyboard and screen-reader review — and deliver a prioritized remediation report you can hand directly to your engineers. Most audits take 1–2 weeks depending on surface area.

What's your rate?

I work on fixed-scope project pricing for builds and a day rate for audits and consulting. Day rate is $1,400 USD with a three-day minimum. Project pricing depends on scope — share what you're building in the form above and I'll send a written estimate within 48 hours.

Will you sign an NDA?

Absolutely. I'm happy to sign a mutual NDA before we share anything sensitive. I work with sensitive product and security information regularly and treat client confidentiality as table stakes.

Do you mentor or do coffee chats?

I do — I currently mentor through ADPList and Built by Girls, and I keep two 30-minute slots open each week for early-career frontend folks. Use the "Other" inquiry option and mention mentorship in the message.

Are you open to speaking at events?

Yes, especially for community conferences on accessibility, design systems, or performance. For paid corporate keynotes I have a standard speaker rider I'll send over once we've confirmed the date.
